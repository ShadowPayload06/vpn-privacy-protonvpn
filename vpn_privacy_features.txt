Encryption Standards:
  - AES-256: Military-grade symmetric encryption used for data confidentiality.
  - ChaCha20: Lightweight alternative optimized for mobile and low-power devices.
  - RSA-4096: Used for secure key exchange.
  - Perfect Forward Secrecy: Ensures session keys are ephemeral and not reused.

VPN Protocols:
  - WireGuard: Fast, modern protocol with minimal attack surface.
  - OpenVPN (UDP/TCP): Highly configurable and widely supported.
  - IKEv2/IPSec: Stable and mobile-friendly protocol with automatic reconnection.

Privacy Infrastructure:
  - No-Logs Policy: ProtonVPN does not log user activity, IP addresses, or session metadata. This policy is independently audited and enforced under Swiss law.
  - Secure Core: Routes traffic through hardened servers in privacy-friendly countries (e.g., Iceland, Switzerland) before exiting to the internet.
  - DNS Leak Protection: All DNS queries are handled internally by ProtonVPN to prevent exposure to third-party resolvers.
  - Kill Switch: Automatically blocks internet traffic if the VPN connection drops, preventing IP leaks.
  - Tor over VPN: Allows access to .onion sites without needing the Tor browser, adding an extra layer of anonymity.
  - NetShield Ad-blocker: DNS-level filtering that blocks ads, trackers, and malware domains.
  - Alternative Routing: Uses third-party networks like AWS to bypass censorship in restrictive regions.

Transparency & Trust:
  - Open-Source Clients: All ProtonVPN apps are open-source and available for public audit.
  - Independent Security Audits: Conducted by third-party firms to verify code integrity and privacy claims.
  - Based in Switzerland: Outside EU jurisdiction and not part of surveillance alliances like Five Eyes.

Additional Features:
  - Unlimited bandwidth on free tier (no ads).
  - Multi-platform support: Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, Chromebook, Android TV.
  - Bitcoin payments and anonymous signup options available.
  - Integration with Proton ecosystem: ProtonMail, Proton Drive, Proton Pass.

Notes:
  - ProtonVPN is designed for activists, journalists, and privacy-conscious users.
  - Its infrastructure is hardened against metadata collection, traffic analysis, and government subpoenas.
  - Recommended for use in high-risk environments and censorship-heavy regions.
